**Policy checked:** 2026-08-20

APS currently requires authors to disclose substantive AI use in a submitted paper. This project used AI assistance for theorem exploration, derivation checking, literature synthesis, adversarial review, manuscript drafting/revision, and mechanical consistency checks. A disclosure is therefore required.

## Conservative draft acknowledgment

The following must be reviewed by the submitting author for literal factual accuracy before insertion:

> OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol) was used as an AI-assisted research and writing tool for literature synthesis, exploration and checking of mathematical derivations, adversarial claim review, and manuscript drafting and revision. The author directed the research questions and scope, reviewed and revised the resulting material, and retained responsibility for the mathematical claims, citations, interpretation, and final manuscript.

## Verification detail still requiring human confirmation

The repository records extensive objective validation, including numbered derivation work packages, assertion-based source generation, hostile-review passes, bibliography audits, exact source hashes, full LaTeX/bibliography/cross-reference builds, and visual inspection of the final manuscript. Rev9 additionally records independent checks of the new histogram estimator, canonical IRF formulas, support inequalities, mark coarse-graining, and measurement-chain caveats.

Those records do not by themselves prove which checks were personally performed by the human author. If accurate, the acknowledgment may add:

> The author independently reviewed the derivations and literature claims, checked the cited sources and theorem assumptions, and approved all material retained in the final manuscript.

Do not use that stronger sentence unless it accurately describes the author's actual verification.

## Placement

The recommended placement is an unnumbered Acknowledgments section immediately before the Data Availability Statement / Appendix during the final personal-metadata pass.
