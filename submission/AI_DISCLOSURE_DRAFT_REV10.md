# APS AI-Use Disclosure Draft — Rev10

**Policy checked:** 2026-08-20

APS currently requires authors to disclose substantive AI use in a submitted paper. This project used AI assistance for theorem exploration, derivation checking, literature synthesis, adversarial review, manuscript drafting/revision, mechanical consistency checks, and the reproducible numerical analysis of the published-IRF worked example. A disclosure is therefore required.

## Conservative draft acknowledgment

The following must be reviewed by the submitting author for literal factual accuracy before insertion:

> OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol) was used as an AI-assisted research and writing tool for literature synthesis, exploration and checking of mathematical derivations, adversarial claim review, manuscript drafting and revision, and reproducible numerical analysis of a published detector-response figure. The author directed the research questions and scope, reviewed and revised the resulting material, and retained responsibility for the mathematical claims, citations, interpretation, numerical analysis, and final manuscript.

## Verification detail still requiring human confirmation

The repository records extensive objective validation, including numbered derivation work packages, assertion-based source generation, hostile-review passes, bibliography audits, exact source hashes, full LaTeX/bibliography/cross-reference builds, visual inspection, independent checks of the Rev9 operational formulas, and a dependency-free script reproducing the Rev10 published-IRF ranking reversal.

Those records do not by themselves prove which checks were personally performed by the human author. If accurate, the acknowledgment may add:

> The author independently reviewed the derivations, literature claims, digitized published-response example, cited sources, and theorem assumptions, and approved all material retained in the final manuscript.

Do not use that stronger sentence unless it accurately describes the author's actual verification.

## Placement

Recommended placement is an unnumbered Acknowledgments section immediately before Data Availability / Appendix during the final personal-metadata pass.
