## Quantitative source check

A. Spinelli, M. A. Ghioni, S. D. Cova, and L. M. Davis, *Avalanche Detector with Ultraclean Response for Time-Resolved Photon Counting*, IEEE Journal of Quantum Electronics 34, 817–821 (1998), DOI `10.1109/3.668769`.

Verified from the full paper:

- Fig. 3 presents normalized IRFs for the DJ-SPAD, Hamamatsu MCP, and EG&G SLiK SPAD on common axes.
- Table I reports FWHM `35 ps` for the DJ-SPAD and `25 ps` for the MCP.
- The text emphasizes that MCP responses can contain irregular bumps/tails and that SPAD diffusion tails can be exponential.
- Rev10 digitizes only the published normalized DJ-SPAD and MCP curves and clearly labels the result as approximate graphical digitization rather than raw-event reanalysis.

No novelty claim depends on the exact digitized values. The quantitative example is illustrative support for the already-proven statement that full timing-law shape contains information not fixed by FWHM.
