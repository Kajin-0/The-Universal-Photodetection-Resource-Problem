# WP27 — Leakage-corrected boundary synthesis/action law for approximate exchange

## Status

**New finite-dimensional robustness theorem proved for the rank-changing zero-radius regime under locally stationary approximate Bohr-gap/exchange structure.**

This complements WP25, which treated finite-radius survival. WP27 shows that the boundary synthesis/action theorem is also quantitatively stable to detuning and off-resonant tangent leakage.

The exact-gap WP18 coefficients are recovered continuously as the commutator residuals vanish.

## 1. Single-Hamiltonian pure-boundary setup

Let `H` be finite dimensional and let

`[rho_0,H]=0`.

Let

`P=supp(rho_0)`, `Q=I-P`,

and consider a pure-boundary complex tangent

`PAP=0`, `QAQ=0`.

Write

`X=QAP`,

`Y=QA^dagger P`,

so

`A=X+Y^dagger`.

The two real derivatives are

`D_x=(A+A^dagger)/2`,

`D_y=(A-A^dagger)/(2i)`.

For any two-sided `C^2` physical family with this tangent, define

`C_Delta=Q(partial_x^2+partial_y^2)rho(0)Q`.

Second-order positivity gives

`C_Delta >= X rho_0^+ X^dagger + Y rho_0^+ Y^dagger`.

Define the orientation norms

`J_+=Tr(X rho_0^+ X^dagger)`,

`J_-=Tr(Y rho_0^+ Y^dagger)`.

## 2. Approximate target frequency and residuals

Fix a nominal positive angular frequency `nu>0` and a detuning window `0<delta<nu`.

The positive-orientation residual is

`R_+=[H,A]-hbar nu A`.

Because right multiplication by `rho_0^+` selects target-support columns, its weighted norm sees only `X`:

`eta_+^2 := Tr(R_+ rho_0^+ R_+^dagger)`

`=sum_(m in Q,n in P)
 |E_m-E_n-hbar nu|^2 |X_mn|^2/p_n`.

For the opposite orientation use

`R_-=[H,A^dagger]+hbar nu A^dagger`,

`eta_-^2 := Tr(R_- rho_0^+ R_-^dagger)`

`=sum_(m in Q,n in P)
 |E_m-E_n+hbar nu|^2 |Y_mn|^2/p_n`.

Here the joint eigenbasis of `rho_0,H` has support probabilities `p_n>0`.

## 3. Near/off decomposition

Define the near-resonant matrix-element components

`X_near` by retaining only entries with

`|E_m-E_n-hbar nu|<hbar delta`,

and

`Y_near` by retaining only entries with

`|E_m-E_n+hbar nu|<hbar delta`.

Let `X_off=X-X_near`, `Y_off=Y-Y_near`.

Define corresponding weighted norms `J_{+,near}`, `J_{+,off}`, `J_{-,near}`, `J_{-,off}`.

The residuals give immediately

`boxed:
J_{+,off} <= eta_+^2/(hbar^2 delta^2),`

`boxed:
J_{-,off} <= eta_-^2/(hbar^2 delta^2).`

## 4. Near-resonant endpoint-incidence projectors

Choose a common eigenbasis of `rho_0,H`. Let `Pi_{+,delta}` be the diagonal projector onto all target-kernel energy eigenspaces containing a row index `m` for which `X_near,mn !=0` for at least one support source `n`.

Likewise let `Pi_{-,delta}` project onto all kernel energy eigenspaces reached by `Y_near`.

If an energy eigenspace participates in both roles it is counted in both projectors. This is intentional endpoint-role incidence, as in the exact WP19 construction.

Define the near-endpoint population Laplacians

`Delta T_+^(delta)=Tr(Pi_{+,delta} C_Delta)`,

`Delta T_-^(delta)=Tr(Pi_{-,delta} C_Delta)`.

Since `rho_0` is diagonal in the chosen energy basis, tracing the PSD-cone inequality over a diagonal range projector gives row-wise squared amplitudes with no interference. Therefore

`boxed:
J_{+,near} <= Delta T_+^(delta),`

`boxed:
J_{-,near} <= Delta T_-^(delta).`

## 5. Arbitrary finite-copy common-record bound

For a POVM, the complex score vector generated by `A` splits linearly into the four score vectors from

`X_near`, `X_off`, `Y_near^dagger`, `Y_off^dagger`.

Outcome-space Minkowski and the right-supported weighted Cauchy–Schwarz inequality give, for one copy,

`sqrt(Tr F_1^tan)
 <= sqrt(J_{+,near})+sqrt(J_{-,near})
   +sqrt(J_{+,off})+sqrt(J_{-,off}).`

Every component is support-to-kernel and trace zero. For `N` independently encoded copies, the weighted norms scale exactly by `N` even for an arbitrary collective POVM. Hence

`boxed:
sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)
 <= sqrt(Delta T_+^(delta))
   +sqrt(Delta T_-^(delta))
   +epsilon_delta,`

where

`boxed:
epsilon_delta := eta_+/(hbar delta)+eta_-/(hbar delta).`

Equivalently,

`boxed:
[sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-epsilon_delta]_+^2
 <= [sqrt(Delta T_+^(delta))+sqrt(Delta T_-^(delta))]^2,`

where `[z]_+=max(z,0)`.

Using `(sqrt a+sqrt b)^2<=2(a+b)`,

`boxed:
Delta T_+^(delta)+Delta T_-^(delta)
 >= (1/2)[sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-epsilon_delta]_+^2.`

This is the approximate-gap bilateral boundary-synthesis law.

## 6. One-sided specialization

If `Y=0`, only the positive orientation is present. Then

`sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)
 <= sqrt(Delta T_+^(delta))+eta_+/(hbar delta)`,

so

`boxed:
Delta T_+^(delta)
 >= [sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-eta_+/(hbar delta)]_+^2.`

There is no bilateral factor of `1/2`.

## 7. Near-resonant nominal synthesis action

Define the positive near-resonant endpoint-incidence action

`A_H,delta^(2)
 := [hbar(nu-delta)/4]
    [Delta T_+^(delta)+Delta T_-^(delta)].`

Every transition counted by the near projectors has an energy-change magnitude at least `hbar(nu-delta)` in the intended orientation, so this is a conservative nominal spectral price.

The bilateral theorem becomes

`boxed:
A_H,delta^(2)
 >= [hbar(nu-delta)/8]
    [sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-epsilon_delta]_+^2.`

For one-sided synthesis,

`boxed:
A_H,delta^(2)
 >= [hbar(nu-delta)/4]
    [sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-eta_+/(hbar delta)]_+^2.`

The window `delta` can be optimized against the residual penalty.

## 8. Locally stationary autonomous clock–signal exchange

Let `H_C,H_S` act on separate subsystems and assume the baseline is locally stationary,

`[rho_0,H_C]=[rho_0,H_S]=0`.

The target complex tangent need only be approximately an exchange at nominal `nu`.

### Signal residuals

Positive `X` orientation:

`R_{S,+}=[H_S,A]-hbar nu A`,

`eta_{S,+}^2=Tr(R_{S,+}rho_0^+R_{S,+}^dagger)`.

Opposite `Y` orientation:

`R_{S,-}=[H_S,A^dagger]+hbar nu A^dagger`,

with weighted norm `eta_{S,-}`.

### Clock residuals

Because the intended clock exchange has the opposite sign, use

`R_{C,+}=[H_C,A]+hbar nu A`

for the `X` orientation and

`R_{C,-}=[H_C,A^dagger]-hbar nu A^dagger`

for the `Y` orientation.

Construct the four near-endpoint incidence projectors from the corresponding local energy differences and define local actions

`A_S,delta_S^(2)
 =[hbar(nu-delta_S)/4]
  [Delta T_{S,+}+Delta T_{S,-}]`,

`A_C,delta_C^(2)
 =[hbar(nu-delta_C)/4]
  [Delta T_{C,+}+Delta T_{C,-}]`.

Define

`epsilon_S=eta_{S,+}/(hbar delta_S)+eta_{S,-}/(hbar delta_S)`,

`epsilon_C=eta_{C,+}/(hbar delta_C)+eta_{C,-}/(hbar delta_C)`.

Then every finite-copy collective POVM obeys

`boxed:
A_S,delta_S^(2)+A_C,delta_C^(2)
 >= (hbar/8){
 (nu-delta_S)[sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-epsilon_S]_+^2
 +(nu-delta_C)[sqrt(Tr F_N^tan/N)-epsilon_C]_+^2
 }.`

This is the leakage-corrected autonomous bilateral action law.

For a one-sided tangent, replace each local `1/8` coefficient by `1/4` and use only the residual of the present orientation.

## 9. Exact-exchange limit

If the intended local exchange is exact,

`eta_{S,+}=eta_{S,-}=eta_{C,+}=eta_{C,-}=0`.

For sufficiently small `delta`, the near projectors reduce to the exact endpoint-role projectors. Taking `delta downarrow0` gives

bilateral:

`A_S^(2)+A_C^(2)
 >= (hbar nu/4) Tr F_N^tan/N`,

one-sided:

`A_S^(2)+A_C^(2)
 >= (hbar nu/2) Tr F_N^tan/N`,

recovering WP18 exactly.

Thus the sharp exact coefficients are stable limits of the approximate-exchange theorem rather than isolated algebraic accidents.

## 10. Interpretation

At a rank-changing boundary, detuning affects the theorem at the **score-amplitude level** before the bilateral orientations are combined.

The near-resonant score amplitudes are paid by positive endpoint synthesis curvature. The off-resonant score amplitudes are paid by the commutator residual. This is why the robust correction is subtractive inside a square root rather than an additive correction to Fisher information itself.

The theorem addresses:

- anharmonicity;
- weak off-resonant coupling;
- imperfect clock-signal gap matching;
- finite-frequency-window assignment of endpoint roles.

It does not yet model dissipative parameter-dependent dynamics.

Parameter-independent noise **after** encoding can only reduce accessible Fisher information by data processing, so it does not invalidate the necessary-resource direction. Noise acting during the parameter-generation mechanism requires a separate open-system tangent analysis.

## 11. Scope and limitations

1. Finite dimensional.
2. Local stationarity `[rho_0,H_C]=[rho_0,H_S]=0` is used to make the near/off decomposition scalar and row-wise.
3. The incidence projectors depend on the nominal frequency window and on which endpoint energy sectors are actually reached by the tangent.
4. The action is a positive nominal endpoint-incidence resource, not thermodynamic work.
5. The arbitrary coherent/history-state analogue of WP06/WP19 remains open.
6. Parameter-dependent decoherence during encoding remains open.
