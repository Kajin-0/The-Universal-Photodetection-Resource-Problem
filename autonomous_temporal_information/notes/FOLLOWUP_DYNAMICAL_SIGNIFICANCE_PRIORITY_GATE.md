## 1. Candidate central contribution

The paper's strongest defensible statement is:

> For a rank-changing local state model with a prescribed feasible metric-contracted target-kernel Hessian `C`, the exact minimum state-weighted quadratic coupling over smooth unitary dilations is `(1/2)Tr C`; the same minimum can be attained under exact total-energy conservation, including in separable infinite dimension under finite-information regularity. In a clean autonomous temporal exchange this gives `V_min=A_ex/(hbar nu)`.

This is materially stronger than the first-order statement

`V_min=(1/4)Tr H_SLD`,

which is an established Bures/QFI horizontal-lift fact and should not be used as the novelty hook.

## 2. Why the theorem is not a trivial restatement of QFI

A hostile referee may argue that the result is only another purification minimization.

The response must be precise.

First-order Bures/QFI geometry prescribes only the tangent and minimizes its horizontal norm. It fixes the minimal curvature generated by that tangent but does not, by itself, solve the problem in which a **nonminimal positive kernel Hessian is independently prescribed**.

The follow-up theorem treats the local data

`(rho_0, D_j, C)`

with

`C>=C_min(D,rho_0)`.

It proves both:

1. an exact lower bound determined by the **actual prescribed second-order target population**;
2. a constructive dilation realizing every feasible excess positive curvature at exactly its additional cost while leaving the first derivative unchanged.

The energy-conserving construction further has to respect target-energy sectors, including spectator curvature in target-energy shells unoccupied at baseline.

Thus the nontrivial step is the **second-order constrained extension and exact realizability**, not the known first-order horizontal metric.

## 3. Why the theorem is not a new Stinespring theorem

Covariant Stinespring dilation is established mathematics.

Scutaru, *Reports on Mathematical Physics* **16**, 79--87 (1979), proves a Stinespring-type theorem for covariant completely positive maps.

Faist, Berta, and Brandao, *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **384**, 1709--1750 (2021), state explicitly that time-covariant processes admit a Stinespring dilation with an energy-conserving unitary and an environment Hamiltonian, citing Scutaru, Keyl--Werner, and Marvian.

Fujiwara--Imai and later channel-metrology work likewise develop fibre-bundle/Kraus-gauge optimization of channel Fisher information and minimization over differentiable output purifications.

Therefore the paper must not claim novelty for the existence of a symmetric/energy-conserving dilation or for minimization over purification gauges.

The candidate distinction is **state-specific local second-order interpolation at fixed reduced 2-jet and exact quadratic cost**.

## 5. Second-order cone mathematics is not novel

The positivity constraint

`C>=C_min`

belongs to established second-order tangent geometry of the PSD cone.

Bonnans--Cominetti--Shapiro and related nonlinear-semidefinite-programming literature develop second-order tangent/parabolic tangent sets and regularity of semidefinite constraints.

The paper should cite that infrastructure and avoid language suggesting discovery of second-order positivity geometry itself.

The candidate contribution is what happens when that geometry is given a **dynamical unitary implementation cost and an autonomous spectral price**.

## 6. Infinite-dimensional significance

The infinite-dimensional extension improves scope but should not be sold as a general new theory of infinite-dimensional quantum metrology.

Established literature already treats QFI, SLDs, Bures metrics, and potentially unbounded logarithmic derivatives in infinite-dimensional models.

What WP28--WP32 add to this paper is narrower:

- bounded-relative / Hilbert--Schmidt conditions that make the survival/synthesis quantities finite;
- an exact trace-class prescribed-curvature implementation theorem;
- a mixed-branch construction that permits an unbounded direct-sum generator while retaining trace-norm `C^2` state regularity;
- semibounded exact energy conservation without requiring the target's occupied energies to be bounded above.

This is a meaningful robustness/completeness result, but probably Supplemental-Material-heavy.

## 7. Physical significance

### Strengths

1. **Closes the kinematic/dynamical criticism.** The synthesis action is not merely a Hessian label under unitary implementation; it equals a weighted squared coupling and, after optimization, has an exact minimum-cost interpretation.
2. **Exact rather than asymptotic.** No large-copy or perturbative inequality gap remains in the cost theorem itself.
3. **Energy conservation is exact.** The cost is nonzero even when the full bare-energy distribution is conserved, separating interaction strength from net work.
4. **Rank-changing/nonminimal curvature is central.** The theorem is genuinely about boundary synthesis rather than ordinary full-rank Bures motion.
5. **Infinite-dimensional completion.** The construction is not a finite-matrix artifact.

### Weaknesses

1. `V_impl` is a **chosen quadratic local coupling metric**, not thermodynamic work, battery depletion, switching energy, or reset cost.
2. The theorem is **state-specific**, not a universal implementation cost for a channel on arbitrary inputs.
3. It prescribes only the physical **metric-contracted kernel Hessian**, not an arbitrary complete second-order tensor.
4. The controller/ancilla Hamiltonian is freely engineerable subject to semiboundedness and exact conservation. This is mathematically legitimate but weakens direct hardware interpretation.
5. The exact temporal specialization still assumes a clean relational exchange for the equality `A_ex=hbar nu V`; WP25/WP27 make information bounds robust to detuning but do not yet give a fully optimized approximate-exchange dynamical theorem.
6. No decoherence/noisy optimal implementation theorem is included.

These limitations should be explicit.
