## 1. Candidate paper claim that survived the audit

The strongest concise statement that still appears distinct is:

> A globally stationary relative temporal mode has two complementary physical-resource regimes. For a nonzero physical affine tangent radius, robust Fisher information requires pre-existing spectral survival on both sides of the clock--signal exchange. When the affine radius collapses to zero but an exact nonlinear physical family exists, useful Fisher information requires positive second-order endpoint synthesis action on both sides. The finite-radius and boundary laws are finite-copy and arbitrary-POVM, their clean coefficients are sharp in fixed-total-energy shells, arbitrary coherent supports require shorted/operator geometry, and the pure-boundary law admits a sharp frequency-weighted multi-gap sum simultaneously saturated by one common readout.

Do **not** broaden this into claims that asymmetry, relational time, energy-constrained metrology, multiphase estimation, PSD-cone curvature, or shorted operators are new.

## 2. Page--Wootters / shared-asymmetry collision

### Prior art

R. S. Carmo and D. O. Soares-Pinto,
*Quantifying resources for the Page-Wootters mechanism: Shared asymmetry as relative entropy of entanglement*,
Phys. Rev. A 103, 052420 (2021), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.103.052420`.

They quantify the Page--Wootters resource using shared/mutual asymmetry, equivalent to a relative-entropy/internal-coherence construction for the U(1) case, and relate the fixed-charge-sector quantity to relative entropy of entanglement.

### Collision

This is direct conceptual prior art for the statement that globally stationary states can contain an internal temporal/reference resource. Therefore:

- global asymmetry is **not** the only meaningful clock resource;
- fixed-total-energy relational coherence is not new;
- entanglement/internal coherence inside charge sectors is not new.

### Surviving distinction

WP18 starts from a rank-one baseline such as `|1,1>` inside one fixed-total-energy shell. At the baseline, both global time-translation asymmetry and the Page--Wootters internal/shared resource can vanish, while a nonlinear rank-changing family immediately acquires useful relative temporal Fisher information.

For the bilateral WP18 extremizer, with

`a=c^2(x^2+y^2)/4`,

the relevant local-energy probability vector is

`(1-2a,a,a)`.

The entropy-type shared resource behaves

`H(1-2a,a,a)=2a(1-log a)-2a^2+O(a^3)`.

Thus, as `r^2=x^2+y^2 ->0`,

`H/r^2 -> infinity`.

The shared-asymmetry entropy is nonanalytic at this rank-changing boundary and does **not** possess the finite quadratic coefficient appearing in the WP18 synthesis-action theorem.

WP18 instead gives the finite operational coefficient

`A_C^(2)+A_S^(2) >= (hbar nu/4) Tr F_N/N`

for bilateral synthesis, with exact fixed-shell saturation.

Therefore the zero-radius synthesis-action law is not obtained by simply taking the Hessian of the known shared-asymmetry entropy.

## 4. Quantitative WAY / conservation-law coherence collision

### Collision

It is established that implementing a local operation or measurement that conflicts with a conserved quantity requires a sufficiently asymmetric/coherent reference or apparatus. QFI-like coherence resources occur in quantitative conservation-law bounds.

### Surviving distinction

The WP18 exchange **does not implement a target operation that violates total energy conservation**. Instead,

`[H_C+H_S,A_nu]=0`

exactly, and the sharp extremizer can live entirely inside one degenerate total-energy eigenspace.

The local signal energy rises exactly as the clock energy falls. The theorem asks a different question:

> how much positive spectral endpoint resource must exist or be synthesized to support a specified amount of *relational temporal Fisher information* in a fully energy-conserving exchange?

The audit did not find a WAY theorem that produces the sharp bilateral boundary coefficient

`(hbar nu/4) Tr F_N/N`

WAY/conservation-law resource theory remains mandatory conceptual prior art and should be discussed prominently.

Their framework constrains the **total energy consumption of the complete metrological protocol**, including probe preparation, intermediate controls, and final measurement, and optimizes the corresponding precision.

### Collision

Any broad claim that energy constraints on metrology are unexplored is false. This 2026 work is particularly important current prior art.

### Surviving distinction

WP18--WP20 use a different resource:

`A^(2)=(1/4) Tr(G C_Delta)`.

This is a local, frequency-resolved **second-order endpoint-incidence synthesis action** of the encoded state family. It is not the energy consumed by a complete physical implementation protocol and, in fixed-total-energy shells, it can be nonzero while signed total-energy curvature is zero.

Thus neither theorem directly subsumes the other from the statements inspected. A future manuscript must explicitly say:

- WP18--WP20 are **kinematic necessary-resource laws** for physical state families;
- Chen--Yang is a **protocol-level energy optimization**;
- the present project does not yet prove the dynamical implementation energy needed to realize a given synthesis action.

This distinction is important but must not be oversold as a proof of novelty.

The sharp fixed-shell Fourier construction shows one common measurement can saturate the physical coefficient, but Fourier/covariant multiphase readout itself is prior art.

The audit did not find the WP20 synthesis-action sum as a corollary of the 2024 linear-waveform Holevo result.

## 8. Why the finite-radius / zero-radius split matters

The audit supports keeping the two regimes conceptually separate.

### Finite radius

A strong local tangent is not enough by itself. Fixed baseline mean energy can coexist with arbitrarily high-frequency local Fisher information if the physical affine neighborhood shrinks.

The corrected resource is

`K_N=(R_lin^2/4) Tr F_N/N`,

and for autonomous exchange

`K_N <= min{T_C(nu),T_S(nu)}`.

### Zero radius

At a rank-deficient boundary, `R_lin=0` can occur even though an exact nonlinear physical family exists and has nonzero local Fisher information.

Then the resource moves to second order:

- one-sided endpoint synthesis: `Tr F_N/N <= Delta T`;
- bilateral synthesis: square-root/Minkowski composition;
- autonomous bilateral exchange: `A_C^(2)+A_S^(2) >= (hbar nu/4) Tr F_N/N`;
- multi-gap exchange: one shared positive Hessian-action budget.

This is the branch's clearest conceptual contribution.

## 9. Hostile reduction tests

The following attempted reductions **failed** in the audit:

1. **Global asymmetry reduction:** false in the fixed-total-energy extremizers because global asymmetry is exactly zero.
2. **Shared-asymmetry Hessian reduction:** fails at the rank-changing boundary because entropy-type shared asymmetry is nonanalytic, scaling as `r^2 log(1/r)` rather than a finite quadratic coefficient.
3. **WAY coherence-cost reduction:** nearby but aimed at implementing conservation-law-violating local operations; the WP18 exchange conserves total energy exactly.
4. **Total protocol-energy reduction:** Chen--Yang constrain a different resource, including preparation/control/measurement costs.
5. **Waveform-Holevo reduction:** addresses the statistical/measurement optimum rather than the state-family synthesis action; WP15 explicitly demonstrates these are separate layers.
6. **Ordinary additive endpoint cost:** mathematically false by WP09's exact factor-two qutrit extremizer.
7. **No-geometry noncommuting-support reduction:** operationally false by WP11's explicit Fisher violation.

None of these failures certifies priority, but collectively they support a distinct theorem package.

WP15/WP16 are supporting evidence that resource bounds and accessible Fisher must be distinguished, but they should not dominate the main manuscript unless needed for referee objections about attainability.
