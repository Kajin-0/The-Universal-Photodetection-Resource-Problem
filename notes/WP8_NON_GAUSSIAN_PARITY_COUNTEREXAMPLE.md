# WP8 — Analytic non-Gaussian parity counterexample to Gaussian free-energy optimality

**Date:** 2026-08-20

## Purpose

The open WP8 question was whether a squeezed-thermal Gaussian detector pointer globally maximizes SLD displacement QFI at fixed nonequilibrium free energy relative to a harmonic thermal state.

The answer is **no**. This note gives a fully analytic counterexample and identifies the mechanism: entropy can be stored inside one Fock-parity sector while the displacement generator maps that entire support into the orthogonal parity sector. A highly mixed non-Gaussian state can therefore saturate the same QFI-versus-energy ceiling as a pure state.

---

## 1. Conventions

For one bosonic mode,

\[
[X,P]=i,
\qquad
N=a^\dagger a,
\]

and the optical/detector translation family is generated by `P`:

\[
\rho_\theta=e^{-i\theta P}\rho e^{i\theta P}.
\]

Write the harmonic thermal reference as

\[
\tau_\vartheta=(1-e^{-\vartheta})e^{-\vartheta N},
\qquad
\vartheta=\beta\hbar\omega.
\]

The dimensionless nonequilibrium free-energy budget is

\[
D_0=\beta\Delta F=D(\rho\Vert\tau_\vartheta).
\]

SLD displacement QFI is denoted `J_X(rho)`.

---

## 2. Mixed parity states can saturate the QFI-energy ceiling

For a Fock-diagonal state

\[
\rho=\sum_{n\ge0}p_n|n\rangle\langle n|,
\]

with generator `P`, the exact SLD QFI is

\[
J_X(\rho)
=2\sum_{n=0}^\infty (n+1)
\frac{(p_n-p_{n+1})^2}{p_n+p_{n+1}},
\]

where a term with zero denominator is taken as zero.

Now suppose the state has support only on even Fock numbers:

\[
p_{2k+1}=0.
\]

Every adjacent pair then has only one occupied member. Directly summing the two adjacent contributions of each occupied level gives

\[
\boxed{
J_X(\rho)=4\langle N\rangle+2.
}
\]

But the general excitation-energy theorem already gives

\[
J_X(\rho)\le4N+2+4\sqrt{N(N+1)}
\]

for arbitrary states at mean excitation `N`; the even-diagonal family does not saturate that stronger squeezed-state energy ceiling, but it **does saturate the unsqueezed variance ceiling** `J=4 Var(P)=4N+2` while remaining arbitrarily mixed.

The key point is that mixing within one parity sector does not cause the usual SLD-QFI suppression because `P` flips parity.

## 3. Thermal state conditioned on even parity

Take

\[
\vartheta=\ln2,
\]

so

\[
\tau=\sum_{n=0}^\infty 2^{-(n+1)}|n\rangle\langle n|.
\]

The thermal probability of even parity is

\[
P_\tau({\rm even})
=\sum_{k=0}^\infty2^{-(2k+1)}
=\frac23.
\]

Condition the thermal state on the even sector:

\[
\boxed{
\rho_{\rm e}
=\sum_{k=0}^\infty
\frac34\,4^{-k}
|2k\rangle\langle2k|.
}
\]

Because this is exactly the thermal distribution conditioned on an event of thermal probability `2/3`,

\[
\boxed{
D(\rho_{\rm e}\Vert\tau)=\ln\frac32.
}
\]

Its mean excitation is

\[
\langle N\rangle
=2\frac{1/4}{1-1/4}
=\frac23.
\]

Hence the parity formula gives

\[
\boxed{
J_X(\rho_{\rm e})
=4\left(\frac23\right)+2
=\frac{14}{3}
\approx4.6666667.
}
\]

This value is exact.

## 4. Rigorous comparison with every Gaussian pointer at the same free energy

`WP8_FREE_ENERGY_GAUSSIAN_CLOSURE.md` proves that for every Gaussian pointer at thermal parameter

\[
a=\frac{\vartheta}{2},
\]

and free-energy budget `D_0`, its translated quadrature noise satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Var}(X)\ge
v_F(D_0,a)
\equiv
\sup_{s>0}
\frac{
\ln\!\left[
\frac{\sinh\sqrt{a(a+s)}}{\sinh a}
\right]-D_0
}{s}.
\]

For a Gaussian displacement family,

\[
J_X=\frac1{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
\]

Use the present values

\[
\vartheta=\ln2,
\qquad
a=\frac{\ln2}{2},
\qquad
D_0=\ln\frac32,
\]

and evaluate just the admissible trial point

\[
s=2a=\ln2.
\]

Because `v_F` is a supremum,

\[
v_F\ge
\frac{
\ln\!\left[
\frac{\sinh(\sqrt3\,a)}{\sinh a}
\right]
-\ln(3/2)
}{2a}
\approx0.2643782569.
\]

Therefore **every Gaussian pointer** with the same free-energy budget obeys

\[
\boxed{
J_X^{\rm Gaussian}
\le\frac1{0.2643782569\ldots}
<3.783.
}
\]

But the explicit non-Gaussian state above has

\[
J_X(\rho_{\rm e})=\frac{14}{3}>4.666.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{\rho\;\rm Gaussian:\;D(\rho\Vert\tau)\le\ln(3/2)}J_X(\rho)
<
J_X(\rho_{\rm e}).
}
\]

This is a strict analytic counterexample.

**Status:** PROVED.

## 5. General parity-biased thermal family

The counterexample is part of a continuous analytic non-Gaussian family. Let

\[
q=e^{-\vartheta},
\qquad
w_0=\frac1{1+q},
\]

and define

\[
\rho_w
=(1-q^2)\sum_{k=0}^\infty q^{2k}
\left[
 w|2k\rangle\langle2k|
 +(1-w)|2k+1\rangle\langle2k+1|
\right].
\]

At `w=w_0`, this is exactly the full thermal state.

Because only the total parity weight is changed while the conditional thermal distributions inside each parity sector are preserved,

\[
\boxed{
D(\rho_w\Vert\tau)
=w\ln\frac{w}{w_0}
+(1-w)\ln\frac{1-w}{1-w_0}.
}
\]

The mean excitation is

\[
\boxed{
N(w)
=\frac{2q^2}{1-q^2}+1-w.
}
\]

The exact SLD translation QFI is

\[
\boxed{
J(w)
=
\frac{2(1+q^2)}{1-q^2}(2w-1)^2
+
\frac{4[1-w(1+q^2)]^2}
{(1-q^2)[1-w(1-q^2)]}.
}
\]

This family connects continuously to the thermal state at zero free-energy excess and becomes the even-conditioned thermal state at `w=1`.

## 6. Squeezed parity family

Apply a single-mode squeeze `S(r)` oriented so

\[
S^\dagger(r)PS(r)=e^rP.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
J_X[S(r)\rho_wS^\dagger(r)]
=e^{2r}J(w).
}
\]

Since `rho_w` is number diagonal, its covariance is isotropic before squeezing. Its entropy is unchanged by squeezing, and the free-energy cost becomes

\[
\boxed{
D(w,r)
=D(w)
+\vartheta\left[N(w)+\frac12\right]
(\cosh2r-1).
}
\]

Thus for a fixed budget `D_0`, the best `r` for each admissible `w` is explicit:

\[
\cosh2r
=1+
\frac{D_0-D(w)}
{\vartheta[N(w)+1/2]}.
\]

This reduces a broad non-Gaussian lower-bound search for the exact WP8 frontier to a **one-dimensional optimization over `w`**.

Numerical exploration indicates this squeezed-parity family can substantially outperform the best Gaussian state at moderate/high temperature. That numerical observation is only a guide; the strict analytic counterexample in Sec. 4 is already sufficient to reject global Gaussian optimality.

---

## 7. Consequence for WP8

The conjecture

> squeezed thermal states globally maximize SLD displacement QFI at fixed nonequilibrium free energy

is **REJECTED**.

The exact global resource frontier

\[
J_F^{\max}(D_0,\vartheta)
=\sup_{\rho:\,D(\rho\Vert\tau_\vartheta)\le D_0}
J_X(\rho)
\]

remains open.

However, the mechanism is now sharply identified: a non-Gaussian state can use **parity-protected entropy** to lower its free-energy cost without proportionally reducing displacement QFI.

This is the quantum analogue of the broader UPRP theme that aggregate thermodynamic resources can hide performance-relevant structure.
