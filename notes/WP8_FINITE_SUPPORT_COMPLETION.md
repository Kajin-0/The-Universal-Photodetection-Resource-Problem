# WP8 — Exact finite-support completion theorem

**Date:** 2026-08-20

## Purpose

The unrestricted harmonic pointer has a UV coherence instability: finite free energy does not prevent vanishingly small coherences at arbitrarily high excitation from improving displacement QFI.

A physically clean repair is to specify the **accessible preparation support** of the detector pointer.

Assume the initial pointer state is supported on

\[
\mathcal H_N
=\operatorname{span}\{|0\rangle,\ldots,|N\rangle\},
\]

while the subsequent bosonic interaction/readout may still act in the full oscillator Hilbert space.

This note proves the exact maximum initial displacement QFI under that support constraint and composes it with the WP7/WP8 coherent-photodetection transfer theorem.

---

# 1. Support projector

Define

\[
\Pi_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}|n\rangle\langle n|.
\]

Assume

\[
\rho_D=\Pi_N\rho_D\Pi_N.
\]

No Gaussianity, purity, parity, or diagonal-state assumption is imposed.

# 2. Exact support-constrained displacement-QFI maximum

For translation generated by `P`, the general variance bound is

\[
J_X(\rho)
\le4\operatorname{Var}_\rho(P)
\le4\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P^2).
\]

Since `rho` is supported in `H_N`,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P^2)
=\operatorname{Tr}[\rho\,\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N]
\le
\lambda_{\max}(\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
J_X(\rho)
\le
J_N^{\max}
\equiv
4\lambda_{\max}(\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N).
}
\]

This bound is **exactly achievable**.

`P^2` preserves parity, and `Pi_N` also commutes with parity. Therefore the top eigenvector of

\[
\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N
\]

can be chosen to have definite parity. For such a state,

\[
\langle P\rangle=0.
\]

If `|psi_N>` is that normalized top eigenvector, then it is pure and

\[
J_X(|\psi_N\rangle)
=4\operatorname{Var}(P)
=4\langle P^2\rangle
=J_N^{\max}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{\rho:\,\rho=\Pi_N\rho\Pi_N}
J_X(\rho)
=
4\lambda_{\max}(\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N).
}
\]

**Status:** PROVED and saturable.

# 4. Coherent optical QFI transfer

For coherent optical displacement input, the source directional QFI is

\[
J_A=2.
\]

If passive source-to-pointer transfer probability is `tau`, the directional SLD-Stam theorem gives

\[
\frac{F_{\rm elec}}{F_{\rm in}}
\le
\frac{\tau J_D}
{2(1-\tau)+\tau J_D}.
\]

Using the exact support cap,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{F_{\rm elec}}{F_{\rm in}}
\le
\frac{\tau J_N^{\max}}
{2(1-\tau)+\tau J_N^{\max}}.
}
\]

This is a finite, non-Gaussian, arbitrary-state detector-pointer bound.

**Status:** PROVED under the passive-linear coherent-displacement model.

\[

\[
\boxed{

# 8. Why this repairs the UV no-go

The UV instability required moving coherence to arbitrarily large Fock index `n`, where the displacement matrix element grows as `sqrt(n)`.

The support constraint removes that sequence completely. `P^2` becomes effectively bounded on the preparation manifold, and the exact QFI maximum becomes finite.

This makes the hidden resource explicit:

\[
\boxed{
\text{accessible pointer excitation manifold / effective Hilbert-space size}.
}
\]

This is not a temperature or free-energy variable. It is a microscopic structural resource.

# 9. Relation to free energy

A hard support cap is stronger than a mean-energy or free-energy bound. The latter allow exponentially small probability/coherence at arbitrarily high excitation; the support cap does not.

One may additionally impose

\[
D(\rho_D\Vert\tau)\le D_0
\]

inside the finite support. Then `J_N^max` can be tightened further by the exact relative-entropy expectation dual applied to `P^2` on the finite support.

A convenient rigorous upper bound is

\[
J_D
\le
4\inf_{\lambda>0}
\lambda\left[
D_0+
\ln\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_N}
\exp\left(
\log\tau_N+\frac{P_N^2}{\lambda}
\right)
\right],
\]

with an appropriate normalized finite-support thermal reference `tau_N` and

\[
P_N^2=\Pi_NP^2\Pi_N.
\]

The pure support theorem above is simpler and does not require choosing a thermodynamic preparation model.

# 10. Physical interpretation

Real detector pointer degrees of freedom are never perfect infinite harmonic ladders with unbounded matrix elements. Possible physical realizations of the resource `N_max` include:

- finite number of bound levels;
- saturation of a collective excitation;
- finite participating carrier number;
- anharmonic breakdown of the oscillator model;
- finite dynamic range of an amplification/readout mode;
- preparation hardware that cannot populate arbitrarily high excitations.

The next step is to replace the abstract hard cutoff by the weakest microscopic matter/saturation parameter that applies to real photodetectors.
