\subsection{Shared-kernel qutrit benchmark}
\label{supp:qutrit-benchmark}

Use a three-level fixed-total-energy shell with ordered basis $\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle,|2\rangle\}$ and define
\begin{equation}
|q\rangle
=\frac12|0\rangle
+\sqrt{\frac58}|1\rangle
+\frac{1}{2\sqrt2}|2\rangle.
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation}
Q=|q\rangle\langle q|,
\qquad
P=\id-Q,
\qquad
\rho_0=\frac{P}{2},
\end{equation}
and choose the exact positive-gap tangent
\begin{equation}
A=|1\rangle\langle0|-\sqrt2|2\rangle\langle1|.
\label{eq:qutrit-benchmark-A-physical}
\end{equation}
An orthonormal basis of $P$ is
\begin{equation}
|e_1\rangle
=\frac1{\sqrt3}|0\rangle-\sqrt{\frac23}|2\rangle,
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
|e_2\rangle
=-\frac{\sqrt{15}}6|0\rangle
+\frac{\sqrt6}{4}|1\rangle
-\frac{\sqrt{30}}{12}|2\rangle.
\end{equation}
In the ordered basis $\{|e_1\rangle,|e_2\rangle,|q\rangle\}$,
\begin{equation}
\rho_0=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/2,0)
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & \sqrt2/2 & \sqrt{30}/6\\
\sqrt2/4 & 0 & \sqrt6/3\\
\sqrt{30}/12 & -\sqrt6/3 & 0
\end{pmatrix}.
\label{eq:qutrit-benchmark-A-support}
\end{equation}

For the support/kernel decomposition $B=PAP$, $K_+=QAP$, $K_-=QA^\dagger P$, the exact weighted norms are
\begin{equation}
J_B^+=J_B^-=\frac54,
\qquad
J_+=\frac74,
\qquad
J_-=3.
\label{eq:qutrit-benchmark-J}
\end{equation}
The minimal shared kernel curvature is
\begin{equation}
C_\Delta=\frac{19}{4}Q.
\end{equation}
For the autonomous embedding, the canonical endpoint-incidence operator has physical-basis representation
\begin{equation}
\frac{G_{\rm ex}}{\hbar\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(2,4,2).
\end{equation}
Hence both synthesized rank-one ranges have restricted price
\begin{equation}
g_+=g_-=\frac{13}{4}\hbar\nu,
\end{equation}
and the minimal-curvature action obeys
\begin{equation}
4\Aact_{\rm ex}^{(2)}=\frac{247}{16}\hbar\nu.
\end{equation}
With $a_+=a_-=5/4$, the mixed resource envelope returns exactly
\begin{equation}
\Psi_{5/4}(247/16;13/4,13/4)=12
\end{equation}
in units $\hbar\nu=1$.

The two-quadrature SLD-QFI matrix for the same baseline tangent is
\begin{equation}
F_Q=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{39}{8},\frac{47}{8}\right),
\qquad
\Tr F_Q=\frac{43}{4}.
\label{eq:qutrit-benchmark-QFI}
\end{equation}
The following subsection proves that the exact one-copy common-record classical Fisher supremum is only $55/8$. Thus this single finite-dimensional model separates physical resource, SLD tangent geometry, and accessible common-record information.
