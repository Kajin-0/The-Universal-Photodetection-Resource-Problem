# What happens after this template is completed

A future agent may then safely create a final administrative submission generator that:

1. takes the frozen Rev6 PRX Quantum source;
2. replaces `Anonymous` with the supplied author metadata;
3. inserts factual affiliation/contact information;
4. inserts the approved Data Availability Statement;
5. inserts the approved APS AI-use acknowledgment/method disclosure;
6. appends the approved Figure 1 AI disclosure;
7. inserts funding/conflict language if required by the selected manuscript convention;
8. does **not** alter theorem/proof/scientific content;
9. rebuilds the manuscript;
10. performs a final source diff, citation check, page render, checksum, and submission-package audit.

Until this template is completed, do not generate a source that pretends the missing administrative or human-verification facts are known.
