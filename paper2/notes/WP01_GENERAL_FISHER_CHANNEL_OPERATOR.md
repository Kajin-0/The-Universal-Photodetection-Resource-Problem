# WP01 — General Fisher-Channel Contraction Operator

**Status:** candidate theorem derived at the formal Hilbert-space level; theorem-grade regularity still to be closed.

## Goal

Replace Paper 1's independent marked-delay kernel by an arbitrary parameter-independent detector channel.

Let `X` be the complete incident optical record, `Y` the complete accessible detector record, `P_theta` the source experiment, `K(dy|x)` a detector Markov kernel independent of `theta`, and `Q_theta=P_theta K` the output experiment. No event independence, one-click assumption, finite-state model, or low-flux approximation is imposed.

## Local score space

At baseline `theta=0`, assume differentiability in quadratic mean along a real Hilbert tangent space `U`. Let

\[
S:\mathcal U\to L_0^2(P_0)
\]

be the linear score map. Then

\[
F_{\rm in}[u,v]=\langle S_u,S_v\rangle_{L^2(P_0)}.
\]

Define

\[
\mathscr S=\overline{\operatorname{Ran}S}\subset L_0^2(P_0).
\]

## Standard output-score identity

Under a parameter-independent Markov kernel and standard regularity,

\[
\boxed{S_u^{\rm out}(Y)=\mathbb E_0[S_u(X)\mid Y].}
\]

This identity is known statistics and is not a novelty claim.

Define

\[
T_K:\mathscr S\to L_0^2(Q_0),\qquad
T_Ks=\mathbb E_0[s(X)|Y].
\]

Conditional expectation is an `L^2` contraction.

## Detector information-retention operator

Define

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_K=T_K^\dagger T_K.}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{0\preceq\mathcal M_K\preceq I.}
\]

For any finite tangent family `{u_a}`,

\[
\boxed{
[F_{\rm out}]_{ab}
=\langle S_{u_a},\mathcal M_KS_{u_b}\rangle.
}
\]

Thus `M_K` is the complete local Fisher-information retention operator of the detector channel on the admitted source score space.

## Universal local Fisher ordering

For two detector channels driven by the same source experiment,

\[
\boxed{
K_A\succeq_{\rm FI}^{\rm local}K_B
\iff
\mathcal M_A\succeq\mathcal M_B
\quad\text{on }\mathscr S.
}
\]

Here dominance means that A's output Fisher matrix exceeds B's in Loewner order for every finite collection of admitted tangent directions.

**If:** immediate from the quadratic form generated by `M_A-M_B`.

**Only if:** universal one-parameter dominance gives nonnegative quadratic form on `Ran S`; continuity extends it to `S`, and polarization recovers the operator order.

This is local Fisher ordering, **not generic Blackwell dominance**.

## Exact cascade law

For

\[
X\xrightarrow{K_1}Y\xrightarrow{K_2}Z,
\]

let `T_1s=E[s|Y]` and `T_2g=E[g|Z]`. The tower property gives `T_21=T_2T_1`, hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_{21}
=T_1^\dagger T_2^\dagger T_2T_1
\preceq T_1^\dagger T_1
=\mathcal M_1.
}
\]

This is the exact general data-processing cascade. General detector stages do **not** multiply scalar transfer functions unless extra symmetry/structure diagonalizes the operators.

## Lost and perfectly retained tangent directions

For `s in S`,

\[
\langle s,\mathcal M_Ks\rangle=\|\mathbb E[s|Y]\|_2^2.
\]

- Completely lost: `M_K s=0` iff `E[s|Y]=0`.
- Perfectly retained in norm: equality with `||s||^2` occurs iff the score is measurable with respect to the retained output sigma-algebra, under the usual conditional-expectation equality condition.

This is the operator analogue of Paper 1's perfect latency-resolving mark.

## Record refinement / accessible marks

If `Y_fine -> Y_coarse` is parameter-independent post-processing, then

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\rm fine}\succeq\mathcal M_{\rm coarse}.}
\]

Analog pulse shapes, trigger metadata, state labels, spatial channels, and other accessible marks are handled as output-record refinements.

## Why this alone is not the breakthrough

The conditional-score identity, Fisher monotonicity, and conditional-expectation contraction are classical. The new target is WP02: show that **autonomy/time-translation covariance forces this general operator to become an exact temporal Fourier multiplier for photodetection**, even when the detector has arbitrary memory and high-flux history dependence.
