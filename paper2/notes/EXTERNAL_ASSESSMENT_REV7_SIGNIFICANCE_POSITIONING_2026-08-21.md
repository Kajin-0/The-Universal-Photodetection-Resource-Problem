# External assessment response — Rev7 significance positioning

**Date:** 2026-08-21

## Status

A careful external-style reading of Rev7 judged the manuscript to be a strong, rigorous, niche theoretical contribution rather than a field-reshaping technological breakthrough.

This assessment is accepted as the preferred significance framing for future submission materials.

## Core assessment adopted

The manuscript's main contribution is not new standalone mathematics in the general autonomous-channel theorem. Theorem 1 assembles standard ingredients — score projection/data processing, Hilbert-space Fisher geometry, and translation-invariant Fourier multipliers — into a photodetection framework that remains valid with arbitrary autonomous detector memory.

Its strongest substantive contributions are the consequences enabled by that framework:

1. deterministic Type-II paralysis can make the complete stationary timestamp experiment Fisher-blind to the homogeneous/DC rate tangent at the classical count maximum while retaining strictly positive local information at every nonzero temporal frequency;
2. the exact high-frequency retention tends to `1/e` at the paralysis point;
3. among all iid recovery laws with finite positive mean, deterministic recovery is the unique law producing zero complete stationary static Fisher retention at the common count maximum;
4. recovery mean + variance/CV + the complete conventional mean saturation curve do not determine the timestamp information experiment.

## Preferred significance language

Use language such as:

- `foundational theoretical contribution`;
- `conceptual high-flux/memory extension of temporal photodetection information theory`;
- `sharp information-theoretic counterexample to saturation-curve intuition`;
- `trajectory-level Fisher characterization of autonomous detectors with memory`;
- `specialist significance for high-flux photon counting, dead-time statistics, and related event-detection systems`.

Avoid unqualified language such as:

- `breakthrough`;
- `field-changing`;
- `transformative technology`;
- `first general information theory of dead time`;
- any implication that Theorem 1's underlying functional-analysis/statistical ingredients are themselves new.

## Comparison with application-driven work

The manuscript should be positioned as complementary to application-driven dead-time estimation work such as Jorgensen & Johnson (2026), which develops efficient estimators in a nonparalyzable/gated setting and emphasizes practical inference. Paper 2 instead contributes a broader conceptual/autonomous-channel framework and sharp Type-II information singularity results.

Do not compete rhetorically on estimator or experimental impact where the manuscript does not provide either.

## Manuscript consequence

No Rev8 science revision is justified from this assessment.

Rev7 already contains the relevant scope qualifiers, technology-neutral dimensionful example, experimental outlook, and conservative prior-art positioning. Source search found no `breakthrough` language.

The paper should remain frozen unless a concrete mathematical, novelty, or submission-stage defect appears.

## Submission-material consequence

When preparing a cover letter or significance statement, center the pitch on:

> A conventional saturation curve is not an information-transfer law. In an autonomous Type-II detector, complete static Fisher information can vanish at the classical count maximum even while every nonzero temporal mode remains informative, and deterministic recovery is the unique finite-mean iid recovery law producing that static singularity.

This is strong enough without stronger impact rhetoric.
