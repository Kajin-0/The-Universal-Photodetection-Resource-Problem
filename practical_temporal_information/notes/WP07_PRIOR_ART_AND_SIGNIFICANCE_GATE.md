# WP07 — Prior-art and significance gate for practical temporal-information benchmarks

**Date:** 2026-08-23

**Status:** PASS WITH NARROWED CLAIMS. The practical program remains publication-worthy in principle, but only if it is framed as a new operational/falsification bridge built around genuinely distinct results rather than as a claim that Fisher information, dead-time timestamps, sideband metrology, or rank-boundary curvature are themselves new.

## 1. Gate question

Could the proposed fourth paper make a distinct scientific contribution after accounting for close prior art in:

- detector dead-time statistics and information;
- random/variable dead-time models;
- inter-arrival-time characterization;
- optical phase-modulation sidebands and Fisher information;
- seeded versus vacuum-sideband interferometry;
- rank-changing quantum Fisher information / Bures geometry?

The answer is **yes, but only with a narrow claim stack**.

## 2. Prior ingredients that are not novel

### 2.1 Dead-time counting and information theory

Historical optical-counting work by Teich and collaborators already covers:

- fixed and variable dead-time photocounting distributions;
- modulation of dead-time-perturbed counting systems;
- information, error probability, imaging, and channel-level questions under dead time.

Relevant lineage includes:

- Cantor, Matin, and Teich, *Photocounting Distributions with Variable Dead Time*, Applied Optics 14, 2819–2820 (1975).
- Teich and Cantor, *Information, Error, and Imaging in Deadtime-Perturbed Doubly Stochastic Poisson Counting Systems*, IEEE Journal of Quantum Electronics 14, 993–1003 (1978).
- Teich and Vannucci, *Observation of Dead-Time-Modified Photocounting Distributions for Modulated Laser Radiation*, JOSA 68, 1338–1342 (1978).
- Vannucci and Teich, *Effects of Rate Variation on the Counting Statistics of Dead-Time-Modified Poisson Processes*, Optics Communications 25, 267–272 (1978).

Therefore Paper 4 must not claim that using information theory, temporal modulation, or interval statistics for dead-time-limited photodetection is new.

### 2.2 Random dead time and interval structure

Random/variable dead-time distributions are established. Modern work also computes interval distributions for arbitrary random dead-time laws in related point-process settings. Inter-arrival distributions are routinely used to identify dead time and distinguish detector modes.

The practical statement “timestamps contain more information than a mean count rate” is therefore not itself a novelty claim.

### 2.3 Paralyzable correlation distortion

Apanasovich and Paltsev, JOSA B 12, 1550–1554 (1995), derive photon-correlation distortions for paralyzable systems with constant and random dead times. Pair-correlation sensitivity to recovery-law details is therefore prior art as a detector-statistics ingredient.

### 2.4 Modern dead-time Fisher-information work

Jorgensen and Johnson (2026) develop Fisher-information-rate / LAN theory for nonparalyzable dead-time event detection with causal gating. This is close information-theoretic prior art and reinforces that generic Fisher analysis of dead-time event records is not new.

### 2.5 Optical sidebands and Fisher information

Weak optical phase modulation, sideband photon counting, homodyne/heterodyne readout, and Fisher-information analysis are established. Examples include weak-phase-modulation quantum metrology and single-photon signal-sideband detection. Vacuum- versus bright-seeded interferometry is also extensively studied.

Therefore Paper 4 must not claim that:

- sidebands generated by phase modulation are new;
- sideband photon counting as an estimator is new;
- vacuum versus seeded interferometry is new;
- Fisher information of optical sidebands is new.

### 2.6 Rank-changing QFI / Bures boundary curvature

The fact that rank-changing quantum models require special treatment and that zero-eigenvalue sectors can enter through second-order curvature is known in the QFI/Bures literature, including Šafránek, PRA 95, 052320 (2017).

Therefore Paper 4 must not claim discovery of rank-boundary QFI curvature as a general mathematical fact.

## 3. What survives as a distinct contribution

### 3.1 Detector-specific information incompleteness from Paper 2

The frozen random-time paper already contains a stronger and narrower result than generic “timestamps matter”:

- every iid Type-II recovery law with the same finite mean `m` has the same homogeneous registered-rate curve
  `r(lambda)=lambda exp(-lambda m)`;
- at the common maximum `lambda m=1`, complete stationary timestamp Fisher information is zero iff recovery is deterministic;
- every genuinely random recovery law has a bounded interval statistic with nonzero first-order response at that same operating point;
- explicit same-mean/same-variance recovery laws have the same entire saturation curve but different local timestamp experiments and different Fisher content under a common coarse-graining.

No direct prior-art collision for this exact theorem stack was found in the targeted search.

**Publication rule:** these are existing results of Paper 2. Paper 4 may translate them into a falsification/characterization protocol, but must cite the companion paper and must not republish them as new Paper-4 theorems.

### 3.2 New practical support-controlled survival-to-synthesis crossover

WP04 constructs an explicit positive-semidefinite carrier/sideband state model with a controllable baseline sideband seed `p`.

For `p>0`, the sideband lies in the support and the finite-radius survival law applies. As `p->0+`, the affine physical radius collapses. The derived crossover identity is

`lim_(p->0+) 4p/R_lin^2 = Delta P_s(0)`.

At `p=0`, the same family becomes rank-changing and the second-order sideband population curvature is the relevant synthesis resource.

This is not merely “vacuum versus seeded interferometry”: it is a controlled statement that the finite-radius spectral-survival resource renormalizes continuously into the rank-boundary second-order synthesis resource as baseline spectral support is removed.

No direct collision for this exact identity or its survival/synthesis interpretation was found in the targeted search.

**Priority remains unverified, not certified.** A broader mathematical prior-art search is still advisable before a strong novelty sentence.

### 3.3 Falsification architecture

WP06 distinguishes three levels:

1. detector-model falsification;
2. resource-inequality falsification after independent verification of assumptions;
3. model-specific saturation/equality falsification.

This is potentially valuable as a practical synthesis even where individual ingredients are known, because it specifies which measured quantities test which layer and prevents a detector-model mismatch from being misreported as a failure of a fundamental bound.

This is an integration/experimental-method contribution, not a new statistical theorem by itself.

### 3.4 Standard Hamiltonian realization

WP05 translates the companion unitary-coupling cost into a fixed-energy resonant beam-splitter benchmark and derives

`V_impl = (t^2/hbar^2) sum_j Var(H_j)`

and, for the two-boson benchmark,

`V_min=(1/2)Tr C=8(gt)^2`.

The beam-splitter Hamiltonian is standard; the candidate contribution is the use of this standard realization as an independently calibratable falsification benchmark for the companion prescribed-curvature cost theorem.

## 4. Paper-4 novelty stack after the gate

The fourth paper should **not** be sold as introducing temporal Fisher information to photodetection.

The defensible structure is:

1. **measurement bridge (background/derivation):** conventional NEP and matched filtering are the linear-Gaussian realization of temporal Fisher-information rate;
2. **timestamp bridge (background/derivation):** ideal Poisson counting and jitter provide the event-record counterpart and agree with the shot-noise NEP limit;
3. **practical application of existing Paper-2 theorem:** standard saturation curves can be information-incomplete in a rigorously characterized Type-II class;
4. **new candidate Paper-4 theorem:** controllable optical support seed continuously connects the finite-radius survival resource to second-order sideband synthesis;
5. **standard-physics implementation benchmark:** fixed-energy resonant exchange realizes the prescribed-curvature cost with independently measurable coupling and population curvature;
6. **explicit falsification matrix:** state what observation falsifies each model/theorem layer.

## 5. Publication decision

**PASS.** A fourth paper remains justified if manuscript drafting obeys the narrowed stack above.

The primary new science should be the support-controlled sideband crossover and the integrated falsification protocol. The dead-time information theorem should be presented as an experimentally actionable consequence/benchmark of the frozen random-time paper, not duplicated as a new theorem.

If a later prior-art search finds the exact WP04 crossover identity or an equivalent theorem, the manuscript must be re-evaluated. In that case the practical work may still justify an applications/benchmark article, but the novelty claim must be reduced.
